# Surface: the converter (index.html)

Mode: Operate. The page is the whole product and its only landing page.

Audience and job: everyday people with a handful of images (receipts, scans, phone photos) who want one correctly ordered PDF, fast, with nothing kept. Task: add, arrange, name, create, download. Proof: the privacy mechanism (RAM-only, deleted per request, no third parties) and the open pipeline explanation, both existing copy. Constraints: pinned brief (dark, luxurious, first-party Apple, Pro-page mood); favicon and name unchanged; no new claims; explainer demoted to a quiet secondary place.

Memorable moment: the upload percentage literally raises the dawn behind your pages.

Unresolved: HEIC acceptance (not requested this round).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a lit stage. Your pages stand on a horizon whose light tracks the real state of the job: night when empty, cobalt first light once pages are set, rose dawn rising with the upload percentage, white day when the PDF lands. It refuses the category's white page, giant red button and card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Depthless black cyc sky; one full-bleed horizon line, cobalt with a bright near-white core; rose gathers above it only while sending; warm day wash only when done. SF Pro on Apple devices, self-hosted Inter elsewhere; Apple local nav, white pill primary, dark filled fields, pages at their true aspect standing on lit ledges. No cards, no boxed content: light and spacing separate things.

STORY: One sentence of offer, drop images, watch them stand in order, drag to rearrange, name, create. The rising light proves the job is real. Below, "Nothing is kept." explains why to trust it; the pipeline and help sit quietly under that.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900. Nav 52px, wordmark left, Privacy / How it works / Help right. Centered 64px headline near y130, 21px sub under it. Stage about 1040 wide from y290, drop prompt and Choose Images centered; pages stand in rows at true aspect on pools of light. Directly under the stage, the dock (filename field + Create PDF pill, status line) whose top edge IS the horizon line; when pages overflow the viewport the dock floats at the bottom and carries the horizon and its light with it, so the dawn is always in view. No step strip: status text and button labels are the text equivalent of each light phase.

## Paper theme (user-pinned variant)

A second look behind a nav toggle, requested by the user as "pen and paper, college aesthetic": a ruled college notebook. Blue rules every 32px, red margin line, blue-ballpoint ink, Kalam handwriting for headings and notes only (body, buttons, fields stay SF Pro / Inter), pages as photos taped in at a slight angle with page numbers circled in red pen. The light system maps to paper tools: a yellow highlighter along the line the pages sit on grows with upload %, each page gets a red-pen check as its bytes leave, and done shows a red check on the status. Theme follows prefers-color-scheme (paper = light) and remembers the user's pick in localStorage; switching is a circular view transition from the button. The notebook rules are the pinned brief, not a decorative grid.
